**Goal.** App-to-app and app-to-datastore dependencies, shared datastores, and operational automation relations.

**Build**
- **Spec `docs/specs/dependencies.md`**:
  - **Network dependency**: from app A to app B, or to resource R, when accepted flows from A's member ENIs or IPs reach B's members, or R, on a listening port. The listening port comes from the ELB listener, target group, RDS endpoint port, or Kubernetes Service port. Threshold: at least 50 flows over the last 7 days. Confidence 0.85.
  - **Load balancer resolution**: flows to an ALB or NLB IP resolve to that load balancer's owning app.
  - **Shared data store**: an RDS or Aurora resource with inbound dependencies from two or more apps, where it is a member of at most one of them. Emits relation `depends_on`, plus attribute `shared_datastore=true` (AK-APP-02).
  - **Operational automation**: a pipeline or job whose `DeployIntent.mechanism=ssm_command` targets resources of app X, confirmed by CloudTrail `SendCommand` events whose targets resolve to X, gets `relation(operational_automation)` to X (AK-DEP-03).
- **Lambda `dp-dependencies`**: implements the spec and writes `relation`, `assertion` and `evidence` rows.
