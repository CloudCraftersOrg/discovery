# P4-09 · Monetization

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Phase | Phase 4 · Process for dashboards |
| Stream | P |
| Depends | P4-07, P4-08 |
| Gate | `review` |
| Paths | platform/processing/monetize/, docs/specs/monetization.md |

Before starting: read `CLAUDE.md`, confirm every dependency is `done` in `tasks/STATUS.md`, run the account guard for any AWS write.

**Goal.** Every finding carries value, band and lag, with a stated assumption.

**Build**
- **Spec `docs/specs/monetization.md`**, and Lambda `dp-monetize`.
  - **Baseline cost per app**: in-scope CUR amortised cost, last 30 days, for members, with membership confidence carried forward.
  - **Unattributed cost**: in-scope cost with no member resource, reported separately. Never spread across apps.
  - **Per-rule monetization**, as `(expected, low, high, lag_days, assumption)`:

| Rule | Expected | Low | High | Lag | Assumption |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-INF-RIGHTSIZE | Cost Optimization Hub or Compute Optimizer estimated monthly saving × 12 | 0.6 × expected | 1.0 × expected | 30 | Recommendation accepted as proposed |
| R-INF-SP-COVERAGE | Cost Optimization Hub Savings Plans saving × 12 | 0.5 × | 1.0 × | 7 | 1-year no-upfront commitment |
| R-INF-IDLE | 12 × monthly cost of the resource | 0.9 × | 1.0 × | 7 | Deleted after confirmation |
| R-DEC-CANDIDATE | 12 × monthly cost of instance plus attached resources | 0.7 × | 1.0 × | 45 | 14-day observe plus sign-off |
| R-INF-LIFECYCLE | Extended support price uplift where AWS publishes one (EKS, RDS), otherwise 0 | 0 | expected | 90 | Upgrade before the date |
| R-INF-LICENSE | 0 expected at finding level; value realized in the business case | 0 | 0 | 180 | Evaluated in the business case |
| Security, delivery and hygiene rules | 0 | 0 | 0 | 30 | Risk reduction; valued in the risk-of-inaction line |

  - Prices come from CUR rates or the AWS Price List API, never from constants. Record the source per value.
- **Moving findings**: `finding_pending` rows get their values and move into `finding`.

**Acceptance**
- [ ] Unit tests for each rule.
- [ ] `SELECT count(*) FROM finding WHERE value_usd IS NULL` returns 0.
- [ ] The sum of app baselines plus unattributed cost equals the in-scope CUR total for the window within 1% (AK-BC-01 precursor).
